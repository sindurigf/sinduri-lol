Minerva: Two Moves

Each row loops one take, 12 drawings a second as on /about. D is recommended; A is today.

Sit to Stand and Back

D. Recommended

She turns side-on while still sitting, then stands with her front paws planted, over half a second. Sitting down lowers her hind end first. All drawings at one scale.

A. Today

The front-facing sit cuts to side-on in one tick, each drawing centered on one spot, the sit drawn smaller than the stand.

B. New Drawings, Today's Timing

The new side-on drawings, one tick each, the same way down as up.

C. No New Drawings

Today's drawings with a squash, a stretch and an eased step.

Start and Stop Walking

D. Recommended

One drawing per step of the stride, so a paw on the ground stays put. Slower first and last steps, and a small settle when she stops.

A. Today

She trots faster than her drawings step, so they skip and her body jumps ahead.
